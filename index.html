Modular Gripper Platform: Achieving 33/33 Human Grasp Types

Bryson Still, Dr. Luyang Zhao | SMILE Robotics Lab | Active Development
Designed a task-adaptive gripper system with 6 modular finger morphologies to overcome fundamental limitations of fixed-design grippers. Successfully achieved all 33 standard human grasp types through intelligent morphology selection and innovative tendon-driven actuation.
Key Achievements:
  • Grasps objects where commercial systems fail (eggs, thin objects, small items)
  • Production cost <$250 per unit (vs. $3K-5K+ commercial equivalents)
  • Complete open-source documentation: CAD, assembly guides, validation data
  • Modular design enables rapid iteration and morphology exploration

Autonomous Tool-Changer: Self-Aligning ISO 9409-1 Interface

Bryson Still, Dr. Luyang Zhao | SMILE Robotics Lab | Prototype V1 Complete
Engineered an ISO 9409-1 compliant tool-changer enabling autonomous end-effector swaps without recalibration. Uses passive magnetic alignment and modular design for rapid tool integration across multiple robot platforms.
Technical Specifications:
  • Self-aligning magnetic gears: <1.6mm concentricity tolerance
  • Tool swap time: <5 seconds | Cost: <$250 production BOM
  • Compatible with Flexiv Rizon, Boston Dynamics Spot, industrial arms
  • Integrated gripper locking and sensor pass-through
