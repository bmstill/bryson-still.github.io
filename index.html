About Me

Robotics Systems Design Engineer

I'm an Honors College student at Clemson University studying Electrical Engineering, currently working as a Robotics Systems Design Engineer at the SMILE (Soft Manipulation and Intelligent Locomotion Engineering) Lab under Dr. Luyang Zhao.

What I Do

My primary focus is designing and developing modular gripper systems with autonomous tool-changer platforms. I work on integrating hardware and software to create adaptive manipulation systems capable of handling diverse object shapes and sizes through intelligent end-effector selection.

Research Interests

I'm passionate about:

  • Tendon-driven robotic actuators and compliant mechanisms
  • Modular robotics and reconfigurable systems
  • Grasping mechanics and task-adaptive manipulation
  • Hardware-software co-design and rapid prototyping
  • Autonomous systems and control architecture
